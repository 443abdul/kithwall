# Kithwall Community Standards

> **DRAFT v0.1 (2026-10-17) — not yet reviewed by a lawyer.**

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Kithwall is a place for the people you actually know. These rules apply to everything on Kithwall,
whether it's a public post, a friends-only photo, a private message or a group chat. They are part
of our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

**Encrypted doesn't mean anything goes.** We can't read encrypted content, but your friends can,
and they can report it to us. When they do, we act on it.

## 1. The rules

### 1.1 Child safety — zero tolerance

- **Never** post, send, request or link to sexual content involving anyone under 18, including
  drawn, AI-generated or "fictional" depictions that look real or sexualise children.
- No grooming: no attempts to build a sexual or exploitative relationship with a minor, request
  images from them, or arrange to meet them for sexual purposes.
- No content that sexualises minors, even if not explicit.

**What happens:** the item is removed, the account is **banned immediately and permanently**, and
we **report it to the authorities** (in the US, the National Center for Missing & Exploited
Children (NCMEC); elsewhere, [NATIONAL AUTHORITY / HOTLINE FOR OPERATOR COUNTRY], and in India,
[INDIAN AUTHORITY, e.g. National Cybercrime Reporting Portal / police]). We preserve the material
as the law requires and never redistribute it.

If you come across this material: **report it in the app, do not share it, do not screenshot or
forward it**, and you may also report it directly to NCMEC (CyberTipline.org) or your national
hotline.

### 1.2 Non-consensual intimate images

- Don't share, or threaten to share, intimate or sexual images or videos of someone without their
  consent. This includes fake or AI-altered ("deepfake") images.
- No sextortion (demanding money, images or anything else under threat of sharing images).

**What happens:** removal as quickly as possible (and in any case within the time the law requires,
e.g. [48 hours under the US TAKE IT DOWN Act; 24 hours under India's IT Rules — LAWYER: confirm]),
and an immediate ban for sharing or sextortion.

If it's an image of **you**, you can ask us to remove it even if you're not a Kithwall user: email
[ABUSE CONTACT EMAIL]. Because we can't see encrypted content, tell us where you saw it (a link, the
account name, or ask someone who can see it to report it).

### 1.3 Harassment and bullying

- No targeting people with insults, degradation, threats, or unwanted repeated contact.
- Don't post private information about someone (home address, private phone number, ID documents)
  to expose or intimidate them ("doxxing").
- No encouraging others to harass someone.
- Don't keep contacting someone through new accounts after they've blocked you.

### 1.4 Hate

- No attacks on people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, caste, religion, disability,
  serious disease, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
- No dehumanising language, slurs used to attack, or support for hate groups.
- Discussing, condemning or reporting on hate is allowed when the intent is clear.

### 1.5 Violence and threats

- No credible threats to hurt or kill anyone.
- No praising, supporting or organising terrorism or violent extremism.
- No inciting violence or celebrating serious violence against real people.
- No sharing graphic violence for sadistic enjoyment.

We may contact emergency services or police when someone's life is at risk.

### 1.6 Suicide and self-harm

- Don't encourage, instruct or promote suicide, self-harm or eating disorders.
- Talking about your own experiences and supporting others is welcome.

### 1.7 Sexual content and nudity (adults)

- No sexual content involving anyone who hasn't consented.
- When Public posting is available, no sexually explicit content in public posts, profile pictures,
  public Pages, Open Groups or public events.
- Adults sharing consensual content privately with other consenting adults is their own business,
  but sending unwanted sexual content to anyone is harassment.

### 1.8 Impersonation and fake accounts

- Use one personal account. Don't pretend to be another person, a brand or an organisation.
- Parody is allowed if clearly labelled.
- Don't create accounts to get around a block, suspension or ban.

### 1.9 Spam, scams and manipulation

- No bulk or repetitive unwanted messages, friend requests, pokes, invites or posts.
- No phishing, scams, fake giveaways, pyramid schemes or attempts to get people's passwords,
  recovery keys or recovery shares.
- No malware or links to malware.
- Don't sell or buy accounts, usernames, invites or Kithville items.

### 1.10 Illegal goods and activity

- No buying, selling or trading: illegal drugs, weapons and explosives (or instructions to make
  them), stolen goods, counterfeit items, forged documents, wildlife products, human trafficking or
  exploitative labour, or any other goods or services illegal where you or the recipient are.
- No organising or promoting serious crimes.

### 1.11 Privacy of others

- Don't post private photos or information about people without their permission when they'd
  reasonably expect it to stay private.
- If you're tagged in something you don't like, remove the tag and ask the poster to take it down.
  If it breaks these rules, report it.

### 1.12 Intellectual property

- Only post what you have the right to share. See section 10.2 of the Terms for copyright
  complaints.

### 1.13 Abusing Kithwall's safety systems

- No false or malicious reports.
- No approving a trusted-friend recovery request without first checking the 6-word verification
  phrase with your friend by phone or in person.
- No attempts to break encryption, access control or rate limits.

## 2. How reporting works in an encrypted network

Kithwall is end-to-end encrypted, so **we can't see your content and we don't scan it**. That means
**reports from people who can see content are how we find problems.**

1. Open the item (post, comment, photo, message, profile, group or event) and choose **Report**.
2. Pick a reason and add details if you like.
3. **Your device sends our moderators a decrypted copy of that item and its content key.** We tell
   you this before you confirm. For a post, the key also unlocks its comments and attached photos.
   Moderators look only at what they need to decide.
4. Moderators check that the key decrypts what's actually stored and that the author's signature is
   valid, so nobody can forge a report.
5. We review the report and tell you the outcome. You can ask us to review a decision you disagree
   with.

**Your report is confidential.** We don't tell the person you reported who reported them. (The
person may be able to guess if, for example, only two people were in a conversation.)

**Reporting is private, but not anonymous to us.** We know which account made the report, so we
can stop abuse of the reporting system.

**Not a user, or can't see the content?** Email [ABUSE CONTACT EMAIL]. We can act on items we can
identify and verify; for encrypted items, that usually means someone who can see the item reporting
it in the app.

**Block** anyone you don't want contact with. Our database stops blocked people reading or writing

**In danger right now?** Contact your local emergency services first.

## 3. What we do: the enforcement ladder

We consider how serious the violation is, the harm caused, intent, and the account's history.

| Step | Action | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No action / guidance | Report not upheld, or content is allowed |
| 1 | Remove the item + warning | First minor violation (e.g. mild spam, one-off insult) |
| 2 | Remove + feature limit (e.g. no posting, messaging or friend requests for 1–7 days) | Repeated minor violations |
| 3 | Temporary suspension (7–30 days) | Serious or continued violations (harassment campaign, hate) |
| 4 | Permanent ban | Severe violations, or continuing after suspension |
| — | **Immediate permanent ban + report to authorities** | Child sexual abuse material or grooming; sextortion; credible threat to life; terrorism |

Warnings and strikes expire after **[STRIKE EXPIRY, e.g. 12 months]** if there are no further
violations.

**Every decision includes:** what we did, which rule it relates to, whether it came from a report,
and how to appeal (unless telling you would put someone at risk or the law forbids it).

**Appeals:** you can appeal within 6 months. A different moderator (or the owner) reviews it,
usually within [APPEAL SLA, e.g. 7] days. See section 8.4 of the Terms.
